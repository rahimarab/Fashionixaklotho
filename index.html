A transparent project pathway

How we work

Clear communication and agreed checkpoints help keep buyer requirements, partner quotations and project decisions organized.

01

Share your brief

Send your design or tech pack, fabric preferences, sizes, quantity, target price and delivery destination.

02

Source & compare

We review the requirements and request feasibility and quotation information from suitable independent partners.

03

Sample & approve

Where available, we coordinate samples and specification review so the buyer can assess and approve before bulk commitments.

04

Coordinate production

Once terms and approvals are agreed, we follow up on production milestones, quality coordination and shipping arrangements.
